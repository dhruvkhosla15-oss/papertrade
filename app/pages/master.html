All Weeks

WeekBook P&LCommissionBook TotalOwner's %Owner's ShareYour Share

How it works: a week runs Monday to Sunday. At Sunday 11:59 pm every open position is valued, each user's result for the week is booked and his coins go back to the allocated amount. Your book is your users' P&L turned round (when they lose, the book gains) plus the commission they paid. The owner takes his percentage of the book total, of the P&L and of the commission alike; the rest is yours. Book P&L + Commission = Book Total = Owner's Share + Your Share. A finished week is fixed.
