Notice To Users

Every user sees this once as a pop-up the next time they open the app. Posting a new notice shows it to everyone again.

Scheduled maintenance

Everyone using the app sees an amber notice and a one-time pop-up from now until the time you set, then a red "Under maintenance" bar while the server is away.

Daily safety check

Every day at 10:05 am and 9:35 pm the site attacks itself with two hidden test accounts — nonsense numbers, cheap-margin orders, many orders at once, instant round trips, touching other accounts, trading closed markets — then checks every real account's books. The result also goes to Telegram.

Backups & activity

A snapshot of all accounts, orders and ledgers is saved on the server every day and kept for 14 days. Download a copy to keep one off the server as well.

Two-factor login (this account)

Adds a 6-digit code from Google Authenticator / Authy / 1Password to your master login. Strongly recommended — the master panel controls every user's coins.

1. Open Google Authenticator (or Authy / 1Password) → + → Scan a QR code, and scan this. Scanning with the plain camera app often does nothing.
2. Doing this on your phone? Tap here to add it directly — no scanning needed.
3. Or and paste it in the app under “Enter a setup key”.
4. Type the 6-digit code the app shows to finish.
