Two-factor login (this account)

Adds a 6-digit code from Google Authenticator / Authy / 1Password to your master login. Strongly recommended — the master panel controls every trainee's coins.

1. Open Google Authenticator (or Authy / 1Password) → + → Scan a QR code, and scan this. Scanning with the plain camera app often does nothing.
2. Doing this on your phone? Tap here to add it directly — no scanning needed.
3. Or and paste it in the app under “Enter a setup key”.
4. Type the 6-digit code the app shows to finish.
